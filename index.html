F-1 VISA • OPT EXPLAINED

What is F-1 OPT (Optional Practical Training)?

Optional Practical Training (OPT) allows full-time employment for international graduates on an F-1 visa in their field of study.

No Employer Sponsorship or Visa Costs:

Hiring an F-1 graduate on initial OPT requires zero H-1B sponsorship, no visa petition fees, and no legal sponsorship burden on the employer.

Direct USCIS Work Authorization:

Employment authorization (EAD card) is issued directly to the graduate by USCIS through the university international student office.

Seamless Onboarding:

Standard I-9 employment verification upon starting (June 2027) — simple, fast, and fully legal.
